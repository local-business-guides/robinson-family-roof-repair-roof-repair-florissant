From roof concern to repair plan

A roof repair discussion usually starts with your description of the issue and a review of the affected area. Share when you first noticed the problem, where it appears, and whether it has changed. The provider can then explain what they observed and outline possible next steps. Don't assume that a visible mark tells the whole story; ask whether the proposed work addresses the cause as well as the visible damage.

Before agreeing to work, check that the written scope identifies the repair area, the basic materials, and the expected timing. Ask how the provider will handle any additional work that may become necessary. A few terms you may hear:

- Roof decking: The base layer beneath the roof covering.
