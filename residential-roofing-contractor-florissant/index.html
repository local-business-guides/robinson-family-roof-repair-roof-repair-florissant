What residential roofing work involves

The work depends on the roof and the service needed. A repair addresses a specific area or problem. Replacement means taking off an existing roof system and putting in a new one. Installation is for a new roof, and maintenance is intended to care for an existing roof. A contractor can explain the appropriate scope after learning about the project.

A few roofing terms may come up. Roof deck means the surface beneath the roof covering. Underlayment is a layer installed between the deck and the outer roofing material. Flashing refers to material used around roof joints and openings to help protect those transitions.
